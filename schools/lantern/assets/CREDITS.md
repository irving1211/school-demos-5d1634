# Lantern Middle School demo: asset credits

All photos come from Wikimedia Commons (checked 2026-11-05). Each one was cropped, converted to greyscale and gradient-mapped with ImageMagick (`assets` built by a local script, not shipped): shadows to midnight indigo #06071c / #1b1d55, mids to plum #45285f, highlights to ember #bb5d2a, candle amber #ee9d38 and candle-light #ffe7bd. One treatment on every photo so mixed sources read as one shoot. Exported as WebP (1000 w + 640 w; hero 1600 w + 1100 w + 800 w phone crop). No child's face is identifiable in any photo.
