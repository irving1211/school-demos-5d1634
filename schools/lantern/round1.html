The short answers

Is it really free?
Yes. No tuition and no fees. Books, uniforms, breakfast, supper and the summer program are paid for by donors.
Who can apply?
Girls and boys entering 5th grade whose families qualify for free or reduced-price school lunch. Lawrence and nearby towns. Every faith is welcome.
See how to apply
How long is the day?
7:30 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Friday, with breakfast and supper. Plus five weeks of summer program every July.
See a normal day
What happens after 8th grade?
We help your child choose a high school and apply, then we stay in touch for eight more years, through college.
See the four years and after

Four years and after

From the first morning of fifth grade to a college campus. Keep scrolling, and the lights come on one year at a time.

5 6 7 8 After

  1. Grade 5 · September

    The first day starts at 7:30

    Breakfast together, then reading, writing and math in classes of 16 (sample). Every child gets an advisor who calls home every week, in English or Spanish.

  2. Grade 6 · The long day

    Homework happens here, not at the kitchen table

    From 3:45 to 4:45, study hall with the same teachers who taught the lesson. Then a hot supper, and home by 5:15.

  3. Grade 7 · July

    Summer is part of the school year

    Five weeks every July: reading, swimming lessons, and a week at a lake camp in New Hampshire (sample). Free, like everything else.

  4. Grade 8 · Next steps

    Choosing a high school, together

    Algebra by eighth grade. We visit high schools with every family, help with applications and scholarships, and practice the interviews until they feel easy.

  5. After · Eight more years

    Graduation is not the end of it

    Our graduate-support director calls every alum every month, through high school and college: tutoring, applications, financial-aid forms in Spanish or English, a ride to the first campus visit.

How to apply

It takes about ten minutes, and we help with every step in English or Spanish.

Who can apply

  • Your child will enter 5th grade in fall 2027.
  • Your family qualifies for free or reduced-price school lunch.
  • You live in Lawrence or a nearby town. We help with transportation.

Have these ready

  • Proof of income: a tax return, pay stubs or a benefits letter
  • Your child's latest report card
  • A birth certificate or passport

Four steps, with this year's dates

  1. Nov 18 or Jan 13

    Come to an information evening

    6:00 PM at the school, in English and Spanish. Supper and childcare are provided.

  2. By March 5

    Fill out the application

    Online in about ten minutes, or on paper at the office. No test and no fee.

  3. In March

    A family visit and a morning with us

    We meet your family at home or at school, and your child spends a morning in class.

  4. By April 9

    Hear from us

    By phone and by letter. New students start with the summer program on July 6.

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this site, in English and in Spanish. It is built to meet WCAG 2.1 AA. If something does not work for you, call (978) 555-0174 or email access@lanternmiddle.example. We will fix it, and get you the information another way within two school days.

Nondiscrimination

Lantern Middle School admits students of any race, color, religion, national or ethnic origin, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or disability to all the rights and programs of the school, and does not discriminate in admissions, financial support or any school program.
