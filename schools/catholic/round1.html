    How faith is part of the day

    We pray together each morning and before lunch. Every grade has religion class, and the school goes to Mass on Fridays and holy days. Second graders prepare for First Communion with their parish.

    Not Catholic? You’re welcome here

    About a third of our families belong to another faith or none (sample). Your child joins religion class and Mass with everyone, and no one is asked to convert.

    Mass at St. Columba Church: Saturday 4:00 PM. Sunday 8:30 AM, 11:00 AM, and 12:30 PM in Spanish (sample).

    Accessibility

    We want every family to be able to use this site, with a screen reader, a keyboard or a zoomed-in phone. We build and test it to WCAG 2.1 AA. If anything gets in your way, email access@stcolumba.example or call (978) 555-0137, and we will fix it or get you the information another way within two school days.

    Nondiscrimination

    St. Columba Parish School admits students of any race, color, and national or ethnic origin to all the rights, privileges, programs and activities generally available to students at the school. It does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, or national or ethnic origin in its educational policies, admissions, financial aid, athletics or other school programs.
