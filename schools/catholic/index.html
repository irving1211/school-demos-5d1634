Will my child be known, and safe?

There is one class per grade, and classes average about 18 children (sample). By October the lunch staff, the crossing guard and the principal know your child by name. Most of our teachers have been here more than ten years (sample).

  • One way in. The front door is staffed from 7 AM to 6 PM, and every visitor signs in.
  • Dismissal by name. We hand each child to an adult on your pickup list.
  • Drills every month. If anything unusual happens, every family gets a text within minutes.

    How faith is part of the day

    We pray together each morning and before lunch. Every grade has religion class, and the school walks next door for Mass on Fridays and holy days. Second graders prepare for First Communion with their parish.

    Not Catholic? You are welcome here. About a third of our families belong to another faith or none (sample). Your child joins religion class and Mass with everyone, and no one is asked to convert.

    Mass at St. Columba Church: Saturday 4:00 PM. Sunday 8:30 AM, 11:00 AM, and 12:30 PM in Spanish (sample).

    Accessibility

    We want every family to be able to use this site, with a screen reader, a keyboard or a zoomed-in phone. We build and test it to WCAG 2.1 AA. If anything gets in your way, email access@stcolumba.example or call (978) 555-0137, and we will fix it or get you the information another way within two school days.
