Layer pick sheet

Every card shows ONE design choice on the same sample page (a small bakery). Only that choice changes, so a heart means exactly that: this type, this corner radius, this button, this photo grade.

Tap ♡ on every option you would want in your own site. Hearts are saved on this phone. Tap "Copy my picks" (bottom bar) and paste the result to me. "Show only my hearts" filters the sheet.

Type family

Display face on the headline and brand, text face on the reading line. Faces with a label job (small caps, figures) show it on the grey line.

Case and tracking

All nine set in the same serif (it has real small caps and italics), so only the case style changes.

Type scale

Same words; the size steps between caption, body, subhead and headline change. The grey strip lists the pixel sizes.

Action form (what a button is)

Primary and secondary action in each form. Hover or press the buttons to see their states.

Surface and material

Ground, panels and rules. Colours stay in one blue-slate hue so the material is what you judge.

Photo treatment

The photo block is a neutral gradient scene; the grade, crop, light and frame change. Real photos will follow the same rules.

Opening architecture (first phone screen)

Simplified phone frames: lines are text, filled blocks are photos, solid bars are actions. Only the arrangement changes.

Grid (columns and asymmetry)

Same blocks (headline, text, photo, action) placed on each grid. Faint stripes are the columns.

Chrome and alert treatment

Where the open/closed status and notices live. The status line is the same words in every card.

Navigation model

How a visitor gets around. The page content stays the same; only the navigation changes shape.

Language switch form

Where English and Español meet. Tap a greeting word in the first card of that kind to see the page change in place.

Voice

The same page with the headline and the line under it written in each voice.

Signature moment

One small static picture of each idea, with the sample page’s colours. The words under it say what really happens.

Motion physics

Live demos. Tap or hover each one. With reduced motion switched on in your phone settings, they stay still and say what would happen.

Spacing density

Padding, gaps and line height only.

Corner radius

Buttons, photo and the small card share one radius.

Ground

Dark, light, or one saturated colour field; the same hue family.

Colour depth

One hue only. Two tints (pale and deep) against four tints (pale, light, mid, deep).

Photo size

First phone screen at 340 wide; same words, same photo, different size.

Words on the first screen

Name, headline, line and one button, counted. Top school sites run about 40-60; ours ran 80+.
