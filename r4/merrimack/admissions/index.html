How the lottery works

Apply by March 1, 5 PM. Brothers and sisters of students are drawn first, then Lawrence families, then everyone else. Not drawn? You join the waitlist, in order.

We never ask about immigration status

We never ask. Every child can apply, and it is not part of the lottery or registration.
