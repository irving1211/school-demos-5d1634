If the day changes

A delay or a closing appears in the line at the top of every page, and we text every family before 6:00 AM. These are the three notices you might see:

  • A normal day (today)Open 7:30 to 5:00 on school days. Nothing else to report.
  • A delayTwo-hour delay: doors open at 9:30 and breakfast is served. Study hall and supper run as usual.
  • A closingSchool is closed: no study hall, no supper. Advisors call every family by noon.
