The lottery rules

  1. 1. If there are more applications than seats in a grade, we hold a public lottery.
  2. 2. Brothers and sisters of current students are drawn first, then Lawrence residents, then other Massachusetts families (sample rules).
  3. 3. Seats in 2027‑28: 66 in kindergarten, and any seats that open in grades 1 to 8 (sample).
  4. 4. Every name not drawn goes on the waitlist in the order it was drawn. The list is good for the whole school year.
  5. 5. There is no test, interview, essay or fee. Grades, language and special needs are never part of the decision.
  6. 6. The drawing is public: anyone can watch in the gym or online.

What is free

  • School itself: no tuition, no registration fee.
  • Breakfast and lunch for every student, every day.
  • The yellow bus for kindergarten to grade 6 inside Lawrence (sample).
  • The first set of uniforms, and the uniform closet after that.
  • After-school program until 5:30 PM (sample).
Apply for 2027‑28Free, no test. The lottery closes Monday, March 1, at 5 PM.
