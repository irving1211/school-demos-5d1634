About Merrimack Commons

A free public school where every family can ask anything in its own language, and every child reads, writes and argues well by grade 8.

Our history

Families and teachers opened the school in 2011 in two floors of a restored 1882 mill on the North Canal. Today 540 students in kindergarten to grade 8 learn here (sample).

Charter and official information

Merrimack Commons is a public charter school chartered by the state (sample). Our charter, annual report and board minutes are public.
