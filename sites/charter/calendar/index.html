Delay and closure notices

On a delay or closing, the notice at the top of every page changes by 6 AM and we text every family that signed up for alerts.

What a notice looks like (examples)

  • A normal day: No delays or closures posted. We post them here by 6 AM on school days.
  • A delay: 2-hour delay: school opens at 9:50 AM and buses run 2 hours late
  • A closing: closed for snow. No school, no buses, no after-school program
