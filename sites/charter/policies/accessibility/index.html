Accessibility statement

We want every family to be able to use this website, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard, captions or large text.

On November 6, 2026 we tested every page type in all four languages with automated checks (axe-core: no issues; Lighthouse accessibility: 100) and checked keyboard use, visible focus, zoom to 200% and small phone screens.

We have not yet tested with screen-reader users or people with low vision. That is our next step, and automated checks do not find every barrier.

If anything does not work for you, call (978) 555-0137 or email access@merrimackcommons.example. We will fix it or give you the information another way within two school days.
