Anti-bullying plan

Bullying is never allowed at Merrimack Commons: in school, on the bus, or online when it affects school.

Anyone can report it: a student, a parent or a staff member. Tell any adult, call (978) 555-0139, or use the contact form. You can report without giving your name.

We start looking into every report within one school day, keep the student safe while we do, and tell the family what we found and what we will do (sample plan).
