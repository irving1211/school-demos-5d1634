Pre-register for the 2027‑28 lottery

It takes about 10 minutes. It is free, there is no test, and you can do it in English, Spanish, Haitian Creole or Portuguese.

Who can apply

  • Any child who lives in Massachusetts and will start kindergarten to grade 8 in fall 2027.
  • Brothers and sisters of current students are drawn first, then Lawrence residents, then everyone else (sample rules).
  • No entrance test, no interview and no fee. Grades, language and special needs never count against a child.

Four steps (sample dates)

  1. Nov 2 to Mar 1, 5 PM1. Pre-register

    Online here, on paper at the front office, or by phone with us.

  2. Thu, Mar 4, 6 PM2. Public lottery

    A public drawing in the school gym, also shown live online. You do not have to be there.

  3. By Fri, Mar 123. Answers

    We call, text and email every family: a seat, or a place on the waitlist.

  4. By Fri, Mar 264. Register

    Accept your seat and bring proof of age and address. We help with every form.

What you need

  • Your child’s name and date of birth
  • Your address
  • A phone number

No documents and no fee to pre-register.
