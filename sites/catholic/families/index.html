Notice

For current families

What you need every week, on one page.

Report an absence

Text or call the office before 8:30 with your child’s name, grade and the reason. Send a text · (978) 555-0137

Pickup

Dismissal is at 2:45. We hand children only to adults on your pickup list; to change it, call the office or send a note.

Delay and closing alerts

We text every family before 6:00 AM, and the dateline at the top of every page shows the day’s status.

Parent portal

Grades, forms and snow-day work. Logins are handed out at registration. (Not built in this preview.)

Uniform guide

Navy sweater or cardigan with the crest. White polo or blouse. St. Columba tartan skirt or jumper, or navy trousers. Black or brown shoes. Gym days: navy sweatsuit.

Gently used uniforms are free at the uniform closet, Tuesdays 2:45 to 3:30, in the old convent porch.
