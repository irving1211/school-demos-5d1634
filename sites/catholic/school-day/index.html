Before and after school

Morning care opens at 7:00 in the library, with no sign-up ($6 a day). After care runs 2:45 to 6:00, with a snack, a homework room and outdoor play ($14 a day or $230 a month). Mrs. Rosa Martínez knows every family’s pickup list.
