Notice

Visit first, then apply

We welcome children from Pre-K (age 4) to Grade 8, of every faith and from any parish. Most families start with a visit.

How to apply for fall 2027

  1. Nov. to Jan.1. VisitThe open house or a tour on any school morning. Book below.
  2. By Jan. 312. ApplyA short form and last year’s report card. $50 fee, waived if you ask.
  3. By Feb. 153. Ask for aidOne form, in English or Spanish.
  4. Mar. 64. Hear backThe decision and the aid letter come together.

What to bring

  • Your child’s birth certificate or passport
  • Immunization record
  • The latest report card (kindergarten and up)
  • Baptismal certificate if you have one (not needed to apply)
  • For aid: your latest tax return or a letter from your employer
