Notice

Faith is part of the day

St. Columba has been the parish’s school since 1928. The church is next door, and the school walks over every Friday.

Every day

We pray together each morning and before lunch. Every grade has religion class, and the school goes to Mass on Fridays and holy days.

Sacraments

Second graders prepare for First Communion with Mr. Daniel Okafor and the parish. Confirmation is prepared with the parish in Grade 8.

Not Catholic? You are welcome here

About a third of our families belong to another faith or none (sample). Your child joins religion class and Mass with everyone, and no one is asked to convert.
