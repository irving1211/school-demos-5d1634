Calendar and lunch menu

Parish notes

The uniform closet is open Tuesdays, 2:45 to 3:30, in the old convent porch. Everything is free.

Sunday Mass in Spanish is at 12:30. School families do the readings on the first Sunday of each month.

St. Columba Hall: $3.1 million of $4.2 million pledged (sample).

Will my child be known, and safe?

There is one class per grade, about 18 children each (sample). By October the lunch staff, the crossing guard and the principal know your child by name.

  • One way in. The front door is staffed from 7 AM to 6 PM.
  • Dismissal by name. We hand each child to an adult on your list.
  • A text within minutes. If anything unusual happens, every family hears.

Faith is part of the day

We pray each morning and before lunch, and the school walks next door for Mass on Fridays. About a third of our families belong to another faith or none (sample), and every one is welcome.
