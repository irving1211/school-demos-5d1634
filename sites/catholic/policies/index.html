Notice

Policies

Three commitments, in plain words.

Accessibility

We want every family to be able to use this site with a screen reader, a keyboard or a zoomed-in phone. Our target is WCAG 2.1 level AA.

What we measured

  • Automated axe-core checks (WCAG 2.1 A and AA) on every page, in English and Spanish: 0 issues on November 6, 2026.
  • Lighthouse on a phone setting: accessibility 100 on the main page templates.
  • Keyboard walk-through: every control is reachable and the focus is visible.

What we have not tested yet

We have not yet run a test with people who use screen readers. Automated checks find only part of the problems.

If anything gets in your way, email access@stcolumba.example or call (978) 555-0137. We will fix it or get you the information another way within two school days.

Nondiscrimination

St. Columba Parish School admits students of any race, color, and national or ethnic origin to all the rights, privileges, programs and activities generally available to students at the school. It does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, or national or ethnic origin in its educational policies, admissions, financial aid, athletics or other school programs. Families of every faith are welcome.

Questions or concerns: the principal, Mrs. Teresa Duarte, (978) 555-0137.

Privacy

This site uses no cookies, no advertising and no tracking tools.

The visit and contact forms ask for your name, a phone or email and your message. We use them only to answer you. We do not sell or share them, and we delete them at the end of the school year if you do not enroll.

To see or delete your details, email office@stcolumba.example.
