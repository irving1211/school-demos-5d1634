Notice

A neighbourhood school since 1928

St. Columba parish opened the school for the families of Lawrence’s mill workers. Today we teach their grandchildren and the families who came after, in English and Spanish.

Our mission

To raise children who read well, think clearly and serve their neighbourhood, in a Catholic community where every family is welcome.
