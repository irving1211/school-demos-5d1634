Policies

Accessibility, privacy and nondiscrimination, in plain words.

Accessibility

We want every family to be able to use this site, in English and Spanish, on a phone or a screen reader.

What we tested, on November 6, 2026: every page template in both languages with the axe-core automated check (WCAG 2.1 A and AA rules): 0 issues found. Google Lighthouse accessibility score: 100 on the phone setting. Keyboard-only use of the menus, the age selector and every form. Layouts at widths from 320 to 1920 pixels with no sideways scrolling.

Automated checks catch only part of real problems, so we do not claim full conformance. A screen-reader review by a person has not been done yet.

If anything gets in your way, call or text (978) 555-0136 or email access@fernhill.example. We will fix it and get you the information another way within two school days.

Privacy

This site sets no cookies and uses no advertising or tracking tools, so there is no cookie banner.

When you send a form, we use your name, phone and message only to answer you. Office staff can see it; nobody else. We never sell it or add you to a mailing list. Ask us at any time to delete it.

On this demonstration site, forms send nothing anywhere.
