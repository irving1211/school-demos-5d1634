admissions

Children from 18 months to 13 years can apply for next fall, and for this year when there is room. Four steps, and we walk you through each one.

Four steps, with dates

For fall 2027. Most families finish in about six weeks.

  1. Tuesday, Nov 10

    Observe a class

    Watch a real classroom for 45 minutes, any Tuesday or Thursday morning from October to March.

    Book a morning

  2. Any time

    Send an inquiry

    The form on this page. We call or text you within one school day.

  3. By Feb 12, 2027

    Child’s visit

    Your child spends a morning in the classroom while you talk with the head of school.

  4. Mar 5, 2027

    Decision and tuition help

    Letters go out together with any tuition help offer.

What to bring

Only at the child’s visit, not before. Copies are fine.

  • Birth certificate or passport (a copy is fine)
  • Immunization record from your doctor
  • Last report or progress notes, if your child has been in school
  • For tuition help only: last year’s tax return or a letter about your income

Send an inquiry

Tell us a little about your family. Lucía in the office answers within one school day, in English, Spanish or Portuguese.
