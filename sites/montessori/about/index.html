About Fernhill and the people here

A small independent school on Orchard Hill in Methuen. About 120 children (sample), five classrooms, one garden.

What we are for

We help children become independent, careful and kind, and able to do hard work they chose themselves. We teach reading, writing and math thoroughly, and we teach in English with Spanish every day.

Fernhill opened in 1987 (sample history) with one Children’s House in a church hall. The elementary classrooms came in 1996, the toddler room in 2008 and the adolescent program, with its garden and farm stand, in 2019.
